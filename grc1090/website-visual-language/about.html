About us

Cutie pies bakery started from a nickname and grew into a dream. Here we have something for all of the cutie pies to enjoy ranging from a fun drink to a good sourdough.

Everything for our bakery is locally sourced. Our fruits and veggies come straight from the farms that you all know and love. Look out for their produce at farmer's market so you can get a taste of what goes into our products.

Everything is made fresh everyday. If you have a doubt, come to the window and watch your order get made the right way.

Come early for the croissants, stay for a coffee, and say hello. Whether you’re stopping by on your way to work or planning a birthday cake, we’d love to bake for you.
