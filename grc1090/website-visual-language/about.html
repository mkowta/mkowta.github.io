About us

Happy Ave Bakery opened with one oven, a sack of flour, and a simple idea: bread should taste like something. Every loaf we sell begins the evening before, when our bakers mix the dough and let it rest overnight.

We buy our flour from a mill on the Central Coast, and our butter, eggs, and fruit from farms within an hour of San Luis Obispo. When strawberries are at their best, you’ll find them in our tarts. When they aren’t, you won’t.

Our bakers start at 4 a.m. The breads come out first, then the pastries, then the cakes and pies for the afternoon. Nothing is frozen and nothing comes from a factory. If we run out, we run out, and we’ll bake more tomorrow.

Come early for the croissants, stay for a coffee, and say hello. Whether you’re stopping by on your way to work or planning a birthday cake, we’d love to bake for you.
